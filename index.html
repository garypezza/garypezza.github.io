About

Leader by day, builder by night.

I'm a Senior Director of Engineering at SimpliSafe, where over the past several years I grew my organization from 4 to 30 engineers across 5 countries, restructured it into six independent squads, and helped drive our app from a 1.5-star rating to #1 in its category at 4.8 stars, now serving 1.3M+ monthly active users.

Before that I was VP of Engineering at GTxcel and co-founded Requiem Software Labs, where we shipped 40+ games and apps to 4M+ downloads. Today I'm also co-founding Zoned Labs, an AI-powered recruiting platform for college athletes.

Across twenty years the work has stayed the same: building things that ship and staying close to the code.
